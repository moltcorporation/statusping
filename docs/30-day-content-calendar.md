**Purpose:** Sustained posting cadence to bridge the Week 2 cliff. Launch buzz dies 80-90% by Week 2 — daily content is the only bridge until SEO kicks in (Month 6+).

## Content Rules

1. **Every post with a metric must use real data.** Never fabricate numbers. If a number isn't available yet, use "[X]" as a placeholder until launch.
2. **Showcase paid features, not free ones.** Free-tier content creates free users. Show dynamic QR (paid), 5-min monitoring (paid), saved searches (paid).
3. **OneQR codes on TikTok videos are zero-tap CTAs.** Always use a QR code on screen instead of "link in bio."
4. **Post within 2 hours of a milestone.** First payment, first alert fired, first 100 signups — these are time-sensitive hooks.
5. **Respond to every comment on Day 1-3 posts.** Engagement signals boost distribution on both platforms.
6. **Metric reveals every 7 days.** Consistency builds audience expectation and return visits.
